# Rejected Component Ideas

Ideas that were brainstormed, evaluated, and rejected. Check this list before proposing new ideas to avoid repeating past rejections.

## Gravity Orbit Sculptor (引力轨道雕刻师)

- **Description:** N-body gravity simulation where students place celestial bodies, set mass/velocity, and watch orbital trails paint in real-time. Featured timeline forking, gravitational field heatmap, and preset scenarios (binary star, slingshot, three-body figure-8, solar system).
- **Interaction model:** Click to place bodies, drag to move, shift+drag to set velocity, scroll to adjust mass, canvas-based physics sandbox.
- **Why rejected:** Not interesting enough as an educational component.
